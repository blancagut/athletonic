Mitochondrial Dysfunction

Mitochondria are crucial for energy production in cells; their dysfunction leads to decreased energy and cellular/organ aging. Malate and Calcium Alpha-Ketoglutarate enhance mitochondrial function and energy output.

Cellular Senescence

Cells that cease to divide accumulate as senescent or “zombie” cells, contributing to aging and tissue degradation. Fisetin helps clear these cells, rejuvenating tissues and enhancing cellular function.

Loss of Proteostasis

Proteostasis involves maintaining cell function by ensuring proteins fold and function properly. Loss of proteostasis leads to diseases and aging. Glycine and Hyaluronic Acid aid in maintaining protein integrity and function.

Altered Cellular Communication

Effective communication between cells diminishes with age, leading to increased inflammation and cellular decline. L-Theanine and Glucosamine help maintain cellular communication, enhancing adaptability and resilience.

Genomic Instability

Increased accumulation of DNA damage contributes to aging and the development of age-related diseases. Pterostilbene and Magnesium help protect DNA, maintaining genomic stability.

Epigenetic Alterations

Changes in epigenetic control of gene expression can lead to dysfunction and aging. Vitamin C and Calcium Alpha-Ketoglutarate have beneficial effects on epigenetic regulation, promoting healthier aging.

Telomere Shortening

Telomeres protect the ends of chromosomes, and their shortening through repeated cell divisions leads to cell aging and death. Pterostilbene and  Microdosed Lithium support the maintenance of telomere length, enhancing cellular longevity.

Deregulated Nutrient Sensing

Aging affects the body’s ability to sense and respond to nutrients, impacting metabolism properly and growth pathways. Fisetin and Malate play a critical role in regulating these nutrient-sensing pathways.

Stem Cell Exhaustion

With age, stem cells’ regenerative capacity diminishes, impairing the body’s ability to repair and regenerate tissues. Glycine and Calcium Alpha-Ketoglutarate support stem cell function and regenerative capacity.

Disabled Autophagy

Macroautophagy involves the degradation and recycling of cellular debris and dysfunctional components; its decline is linked to aging. Glucosamine and Fisetin promote the autophagic process, aiding cellular renewal and longevity.

Inflammaging

Chronic, low-grade inflammation increases with age, contributing significantly to the progression of age-related diseases. Ginger and Pterostilbene mitigate inflammation, enhancing cellular health and longevity.

Microbiome Dysbiosis

An imbalance in the gut microbiome can lead to a host of health issues and is linked to aging. Rhodiola rosea and Fisetin support a healthy gut microbiome, promoting overall well-being.

When and how should I take NOVOS Boost?

Adults—take 2 capsules per day, 1 to 2 times daily, along with a glass of water. As a minimum dose, we advise to take between 250 mg and 500 per day (2-4 capsules) on a regular basis.

To achieve an energy boost, take 3-7 capsules together.

NOVOS Boost is best used daily in combination with NOVOS Core for optimal healthy aging.

Pregnant or nursing mothers, children under the age of 18 and individuals with a known medical condition should consult a doctor before use.

Why should I take NOVOS Boost?

NMN is a building block of NAD+, an important molecule that enzymes need in order to support healthy DNA, maintain the epigenome, and ensure cellular integrity.

The older we get, the less NMN and NAD+ circulates in our body. NMN is one of the most well-researched compounds found in nature to produce longevity effects.

We suggest NOVOS Core as your foundational supplement, and Boost as a “”booster”” to it. However, if you cannot afford NOVOS Core and would like to take just one ingredient, we recommend it to be the NMN found in NOVOS Boost.

Note: not all NMN is the same. NOVOS Boost contains high-quality, high-purity NMN, tested by independent 3rd party labs. Less than 1% impurities are only nicotinamide, NR and water, which are harmless.

What does NOVOS Boost do?

NOVOS Boost contains a single, powerful ingredient that has been clinically and scientifically shown to address multiple causes of aging to support energy, metabolism and cognition as you age. If you did not have the budget to afford NOVOS Core, we recommend at least taking this NMN supplement. You can learn more on our Science Page.

What’s NOVOS Boost made of?

See nutrition label. NOVOS Boost is made of 1 key longevity ingredient: β-Nicotinamide Mononucleotide (NMN).

Other ingredients: Vegetable cellulose.

No artificial colors, preservatives or additives.
Gluten-free. Non-GMO. Suitable for vegans. GMP certified.

When will I see or feel results?

The focus is on the long-term. Remember that the key benefits of achieving maximum lifespan potential may not be perceived on a daily basis, as it impacts biology over time with consistent daily use on your Longevity Journey.

Many customers report seeing or feeling a noticeable benefit within 30 days. However, everyone’s body is different, and the benefits you may see or feel (skin health, energy, mood, etc.) are often just the most visceral ones.

Some people feel results in as a little as 7 days due to specific benefits like skin health and cognitive support.

Other longer term benefits like mitochondrial health and genomic stability may take longer, or you may not notice anything at all. That’s completely normal.

As long as you continue your daily intake, those beneficial nutrients are doing their work inside to optimize your health. NOVOS Boost is best taken in addition to NOVOS Core, our foundational formula.

Who is NOVOS Boost for?

NOVOS Boost should be thought of as an add-on to our foundational product, NOVOS Core. However, if you are not currently able to afford NOVOS Core, NOVOS Boost may be your starting point for now.

NOVOS Boost is meant for early-adult, middle-aged and elderly people. It is developed to slow down aging, a process that already starts from a young age. The most pronounced short-term effects will be noticed by older people, while the greatest long-term longevity benefits will come from starting at a younger age.

NOVOS Boost is safe to take with other supplements.

There are no known contraindications with NOVOS Boost and any over-the-counter or prescription medications, though we encourage you to double check with your physician before adding our product to your regimen.

While there are no known health risks with NOVOS Boost, we recommend starting with a minimum dose of 2 capsules per day and monitoring how you feel.

Pregnant or nursing mothers, people under the age of 18, and individuals with a known medical condition should consult a doctor before use.

Do I need to refrigerate NOVOS Boost?

The main ingredient in NOVOS Boost is NMN. Ideally, you should store NMN in a cool, dry place – room temperature is perfectly fine. You can also store it in the refrigerator. Keep it out of light and avoid too high temperatures for extended periods of time.
