Product overview

Performance starts with the right formula.

When recovery meets performance, you’re left with one thing — MAXIMUM RESULTS. The new FLEXX EAAs + Hydration is specifically formulated for athletes who demand more. More growth. More endurance. More from every training session. This advanced formula delivers all 9 essential amino acids with a scientifically backed 2:1:1 BCAA ratio to fuel muscle building, speed up recovery, and preserve lean muscle mass. Infused with key electrolytes and patented Sensoril® Ashwagandha, FLEXX EAAs + Hydration also helps you stay hydrated and maintain your performance during long training sessions. Recover stronger. Perform longer. Stay FLEXX’d. Build & Preserve Lean Muscle Enhance Recovery & Endurance Replenish Electrolytes for Peak Hydration Key Ingredients L-Leucine Leucine is considered to be one of the most important BCAAs because it plays a key role in boosting muscle protein synthesis, supporting lean muscle growth, and preserving muscle mass during intense training. L-Isoleucine Isoleucine supports muscle repair while also helping regulate blood sugar and energy levels, giving athletes the necessary fuel they need to maximize their performance and endurance through every rep and set. L-Valine Valine works together with leucine and isoleucine to prevent muscle breakdown and support tissue repair, making it essential for recovery and muscle preservation. L-Lysine Lysine is crucial for muscle tissue growth and repair, while also supporting calcium absorption and a strong immune system for well-rounded recovery. L-Threonine Threonine helps maintain proper protein balance in the body and supports healthy connective tissues and muscles for athletes who are constantly pushing their bodies to the limit. DL-Phenylalanine A precursor to key neurotransmitters, DL-Phenylalanine supports optimized cognitive function, motivation, and mood to help stay mentally focused during grueling training sessions. L-Tryptophan Tryptophan helps with the production of serotonin, which supports an elevation in mood and can help reduce perceived exertion during exercise for improved workout endurance. L-Histidine Histidine plays a role in the production of carnosine, which actively helps buffer lactic acid in muscles to improve training capacity. DL-Methionine Methionine helps with detoxification in the body and aids with the production of creatine, supporting metabolic function and overall muscle health for optimized performance. Coconut Water Powder Coconut water powder helps replenish electrolytes and maintain optimal hydration levels, supporting performance, endurance, and recovery. Sensoril® Ashwagandha Clinically studied and patented Sensoril® Ashwagandha helps reduce stress and support vitality for athletes who need to recover both mentally and physically between training sessions. How to use As a dietary supplement, mix one (1) scoop of FLEXX EAAs with 8-16 fl. oz. of cold water or beverage prior to training, during training, and after training for maximum efficacy. Do not use if you have a medical condition or are taking any prescription medication. Consult a physician prior to using this product if you have any pre-existing medical conditions. If you experience any other adverse reaction, discontinue use immediately and consult a physician. Discontinue use 2 weeks prior to surgery. DO NOT EXCEED RECOMMENDED SERVING. Do not use if safety seal is broken or missing. Store in a cool dry place away from sunlight, moisture, and heat. KEEP OUT OF REACH OF CHILDREN.
