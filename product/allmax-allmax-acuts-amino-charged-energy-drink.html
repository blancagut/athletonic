Performance starts with the right formula.

Why Choose Allmax ACuts Amino Energy Drink? Allmax ACuts is a zero-sugar, dye-free Amino-Charged Energy Drink delivering 4.2 g of amino acid energy, 127 mg of natural-source caffeine, 2,000 mg of taurine , 500 mg of L-carnitine , and a full BCAA blend per serving, all for only 10 calories. It supports clean energy, mental focus, and fat metabolism for training sessions, workday performance, or anytime sustained alertness is needed. Standard energy drinks rely on sugar and synthetic stimulants to deliver short-lived energy spikes followed by crashes. ACuts takes a fundamentally different approach, combining amino acids with natural caffeine from green coffee extract for smooth, sustained energy with zero sugar, zero crashes, and only 10 calories per serving. Clean Energy and Focus — 127 mg Natural Caffeine Each serving of Allmax ACuts delivers 127 mg of natural caffeine sourced from green coffee extract ( Coffea canephora , seed). This moderate dose falls well within the FDA 's 400 mg daily safe limit for healthy adults and provides steady alertness without the jitters associated with high-stimulant formulas. ACuts also includes 125 mg of green coffee extract standardized to 50% chlorogenic acids. Chlorogenic acids are polyphenol antioxidants that support metabolic health and complement caffeine's energy-boosting effects. A 2021 systematic review published in Frontiers in Physiology found that taurine supplementation reduces muscle fatigue, lowers oxidative stress, and supports exercise endurance. Additional research shows taurine contributes to hydration balance, electrolyte regulation, and cardiovascular function during physical activity. This makes ACuts a functional amino energy supplement, not just a caffeine delivery system, but a formula that supports actual exercise performance at the muscular level. L-Carnitine for Fat Metabolism Each scoop contains 500 mg of L-Carnitine L-Tartrate , a compound that transports long-chain fatty acids into cellular mitochondria for energy production. This process increases the body's reliance on fat as fuel, supports stamina during prolonged exercise, and helps reduce lactic acid buildup. For users in a calorie deficit or focused on body recomposition, ACuts provides metabolic support that complements structured training and nutrition—without adding sugar, fat, or excess calories. BCAAs and Amino Acid Support Every serving delivers 725 mg of branched-chain amino acids (BCAAs) in a 9:6:5 leucine-to-valine-to-isoleucine ratio, along with L-Alanine, L-Glycine, L-Lysine, L-Glutamine, L-Phenylalanine, L-Histidine, and L-Threonine. This full-spectrum amino acid profile supports muscle preservation during training, reduces exercise-induced muscle breakdown, and provides the building blocks for recovery. Anytime Energy — Pre-Workout, Mid-Day, or Non-Training Days Allmax ACuts is designed for flexible daily use. Consume it as a pre-workout amino supplement 15–30 minutes before training, sip it during a workout for sustained endurance, or drink it mid-afternoon as a zero-sugar coffee replacement. ACuts performs equally well on rest days — the amino acids and natural caffeine support focus and alertness regardless of training schedule.
