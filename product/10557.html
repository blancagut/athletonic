About this product

Schiek L7020 Contour Lever Belt with Stainless Steel Buckle. The Gold Standard in Powerlifting Belts.

The Schiek L7020 Contour Lever Belt delivers elite performance, precision support, and unmatched comfort for bodybuilders, powerlifters, and serious strength athletes. Designed with Schiek’s patented hip and rib contour, this advanced 10mm suede lever belt perfectly conforms to your body, reducing pressure points while providing exceptional lower back and core stability.

Featuring a stainless steel lever buckle and heavy-duty closure system, the L7020 allows for quick, secure tightening and release — ideal for seamless transitions between training sets. Built for maximum strength, durability, and comfort, this belt gives you the confidence to lift heavier, train harder, and push limits safely.

Whether you’re competing on stage or training in the gym, the Schiek L7020 lever belt is trusted by top IFBB pros, Olympia champions, and athletes worldwide.

🔑 Key Features

• ⚙️ Stainless Steel Lever Buckle – Easy to tighten and release with a precision locking system for secure fit.

• 💪 Patented Hip & Rib Contour – Exclusive Schiek design for superior comfort and anatomical support.

• 🧱 Heavy-Duty Construction – Built from high-strength materials for ultimate durability and performance.

• 🧵 10mm Suede Leather – Premium suede finish for grip, rigidity, and long-lasting support.

• 🏋️ Perfect for Powerlifting, Bodybuilding & Strength Training – Ideal for squats, deadlifts, and bench press.

• 🔒 Maximum Core Stability – Reinforces proper lifting form and protects the spine under heavy loads.
