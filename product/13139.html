Thank you for viewing our Bear KompleX weightlifting belt for powerlifting,  strength training, squats, and a variety of other movements... The Bear KompleX weight training belt is designed to support your lifting technique during intense Olympic lifts (Squats, Power cleans, Snatches, Deadlifts, etc) or metcon movements. Our BK belt is perfect for "heavy" weights and quickly comes on/off depending on the movement.
