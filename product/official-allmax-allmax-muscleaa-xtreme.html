Unlike single-category amino supplements, MusclEAA Xtreme combines a full-spectrum EAA blend with a 550 mg nootropic blend, an endurance complex, and an electrolyte hydration matrix into a single pharmaceutical-grade formula. The ISSN position statement on EAA supplementation indicates that free-form EAA ingestion may support muscle protein synthesis more effectively than an equivalent amount of intact protein, and that EAA supplementation can help support skeletal muscle maintenance and performance. Each serving delivers 10 g of active EAA, with the dosage on the Supplement Facts panel reflecting only the active amino acid content, not the total molecular weight of the HCl-bound forms. Nootropic Blend for Mental Focus During Training MuscleAA Xtreme includes a 550 mg stimulant-free nootropic blend featuring L-Tyrosine (350 mg) and Alpha-GPC/L-Alpha-Glycerylphosphorylcholine (200 mg), two cognitive-support ingredients designed to help maintain concentration, alertness, and drive during demanding workouts. For athletes who need both physical output and mental sharpness, particularly during high-volume or technically demanding training sessions, this nootropic blend adds a cognitive dimension absent from standard EAA supplements. L-Carnitine for Endurance Support The endurance blend provides 1,000 mg of L-Carnitine (from L-Carnitine and L-Carnitine L-Tartrate), which helps the body utilize fat as an energy source and may help spare glycogen to extend training stamina. This dual-form carnitine complex supports endurance athletes and strength athletes alike who train through multiple sets or extended sessions. L-Glutamine for Recovery MuscleAA Xtreme delivers 2,000 mg of L-Glutamine per serving, an amino acid that supports muscle repair, immune function, and post-workout recovery, particularly under high-volume or high-intensity training conditions. Post-workout is an ideal time to support recovery with glutamine, as training places significant demand on the body's glutamine stores. Electrolytes & B-Vitamins for Hydration and Metabolism The hydration blend supplies sodium chloride (from Himalayan Pink Salt, providing 7 mg of sodium), potassium citrate (1.15 mg), and magnesium bisglycinate (1 mg) to help maintain fluid balance and support the efficiency of muscle contraction. The B-vitamin complex B6 (1.5 mg/88% DV), B12 (25 mcg/1,042% DV), and Biotin/B7 (125 mcg/417% DV) support energy metabolism and amino acid utilization throughout your workout.
