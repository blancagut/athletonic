About this product

Support a Strong & Healthy Immune System with Opti-Greens 50: The Ultimate Superfood Greens Powder

Opti-Greens 50 can help support:

• Immune Health
• Gut Health
• Digestion
• Natural Energy
• pH Balance

It doesn't matter whether it's January or June, staying healthy is important all year long. As soon as someone starts coughing around us, we're quick to disinfect every surface and break out the hand sanitizer ... but there's A LOT more you can do to protect yourself and your health.

Did you know that approximately 80% of your immune system is located in your gut? Giving your body the right nutrients can help build a strong immune system. Plus, a strong immune system and gut can help improve many aspects of your overall health.

Opti-Greens 50 is an immune-supporting superfood greens powder. Every serving of Opti-Greens 50 is packed with 50 hand-chosen, gluten-free, alkalizing ingredients. It also contains a diverse blend of natural fruits, veggies, and grasses to maximize the nutrients you can get in every scoop of this green superfood powder.

Opti-Greens 50 can be a quick and convenient way to get your daily dose of nutrient-rich greens without the sourcing, preparing, cooking, and cleaning ... the perfect solution for busy lifestyles.

At 1st Phorm, we make quality and bioavailability a priority. If your body can't absorb it, it won't be of much help ... and that goes for anything, including a greens superfood powder. That's why Opti-Greens 50 features low-temperature processed, 100% organic grasses, non-GMO, non-synthetic botanical superfoods, phytonutrients, digestive enzymes, and probiotics.

You'll find a broad spectrum of naturally occurring phytonutrients and antioxidants in our greens superfood powder. These nutrients work to combat oxidative stress and support cellular health to give the body what it needs to function optimally every day. The 8 organic grasses and greens in Opti-Greens 50 alone deliver the nutrients of a 2-oz shot of juiced greens!

Along with the grasses, Opti-Greens 50 is packed with carotenoids and bioflavonoids in every scoop. These compounds can help support overall health and wellness.

To help break down and digest food, Opti-Greens 50 also includes a digestive enzyme and probiotic. Each serving provides over 5 billion CFUs (colony-forming units) from 10 different strains of good bacteria.

This combination in our green superfood powder can help support digestion and overall gut health. Many people even report relief from bloating and indigestion, both of which can be signs of poor digestion.

Lastly, Opti-Greens 50 can help support a healthy glycemic response from food through its Glycemic Balance Blend. Balancing this can help support more stable energy levels, avoiding the highs and lows from spikes and crashes. With more balanced blood sugar levels, improved natural energy can be more noticeable.

Who knew a green superfood powder could help so much?

It doesn't matter if you're exercising daily and working toward a fitness goal, or you're just trying to stay healthy ... Opti-Greens 50 provides key nutrients to help you feel your best all year long!*
