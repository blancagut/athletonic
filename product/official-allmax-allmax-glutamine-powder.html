How ALLMAX Glutamine Powder Supports Muscle Recovery, Gut Health, and Immunity ALLMAX Glutamine Powder delivers 5 g of free-form L-Glutamine per scoop, produced through a Japanese fermentation process using GlutaSure technology. This plant-based, non-animal method produces pharmaceutical-grade amino acid crystals with enhanced purity and bioavailability. Glutamine is the most abundant amino acid in muscle tissue and becomes conditionally essential during high-intensity training, meaning demand outpaces the body's natural production. Key Benefits at a Glance: Pure L-glutamine — no fillers, no additives, no artificial colors Supports muscle recovery and reduces soreness post-exercise Supports immune system function —particularly during high-volume training Supports gut lining integrity as the primary fuel for intestinal epithelial cells Lab tested every lot in a cGMP-registered facility Three sizes available: 100 g, 400 g, 1,000 g What GlutaSure Technology Does Differently GlutaSure is a pressure-driven ceramic membrane process that does three things: it reduces glutamine particle size, increases suspension time in liquid, and speeds glutamine absorption into the bloodstream. The result is a free-form powder with superior dissolution characteristics compared to standard glutamine, making each gram more readily available to muscle tissue and the gut lining. What Does L-Glutamine Do? L-glutamine is a conditionally essential amino acid that may require supplemental support during intense training or caloric restriction. It plays a central role in muscle protein synthesis, immune defense, and gut barrier integrity. A double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled study found that L-glutamine supplementation was associated with faster strength recovery and a reduction in muscle soreness following intense eccentric exercise. Is L-Glutamine Good for Gut Health? Glutamine is the primary fuel source for intestinal epithelial cells and may help support the integrity of the gut lining. Athletes following high-volume training regimens may benefit from glutamine supplementation as part of a comprehensive recovery protocol. ALLMAX Glutamine is unflavored and mixes easily into water, protein shakes, or Aminocore BCAAs without altering taste.
