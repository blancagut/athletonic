About this product

Guess what, we've been progressively upgrading our #1 Best Selling Jiu Jitsu gi to make it even better. New features include 380 GSM fabric and single color pants. 

Just What You Need. We designed the Sanabul Essential Jiu-Jitsu Gi to set the bar for what you can expect from a BJJ gi whether this is your first Jiu Jitsu gi or an addition to your current line up. Quality construction, clean design, and incredible value make this Brazilian Jiu Jitsu gi the #1 Best Seller in the industry 

Premium Fabric. Most brands use a polyester and cotton blend to save money. We dont. Sanabul gis are made with 100% cotton fabric which is more expensive but well worth it because it doesnt stretch during training and breathes better

Pre-Shrunk. No more guessing or buying a size up.  If it fits perfectly out of the bag, just wash in cold water and hang dry. Still want to shrink it? You can! Wash in hot water and throw in the dryer to shrink it until it fits just right

Lightweight & Durable. We've combined lightweight, breathable, fabric with a durable weave construction to create a perfect gi for year-round training
