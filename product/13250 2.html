About this product

Creatine monohydrate is one of the most researched sports nutrition supplements for good reason. Animal Creatine Chews takes this decades old proven supplement and delivers it in a delicious and convenient chewable tablet. Formulated to increase strength, improve endurance, and boost brain function. Animal Creatine Chews are a convenient way to support your training and performance goals, regardless of where the day takes you. 

Easy-to-digest and fast-absorbing, these delicious creatine chews help build muscle strength, size, and endurance without the need to carry around water, a shaker bottle, or even the need to measure out powders — simply grab, chew, and go.

Studies have shown that creatine can boost your body's natural ATP production the main energy source for workouts. As you train, ATP levels drain, but the micronized Creatine Monohydrate in Animal Creatine Chews boosts your body’s natural ATP production allowing you to recover faster from intense training with less muscle soreness. You also will benefit with enhanced cognitive function, giving you the mental focus and endurance to push through your hardest training days. 

  • Speeds Up Muscle Growth
  • Reduces Fatigue and Tiredness
  • Improves Brain Cognition/Function 
  • Improves High Intensity Performance
  • Helps ATP production for Faster Recovery

Delicious and chewable tablets for anytime, anywhere, Animal Creatine Chews are a tasty and convenient treat to your supplement plan.
