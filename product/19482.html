About this product

Achieve a Professional Look While Ensuring Athlete Safety with Customizable Wall and Post Pads from Century Mat Solutions! Proudly manufactured in Oklahoma, USA.

At Century Mat Solutions, we are dedicated to helping you create a clean, professional appearance for your dojo or facility while prioritizing the safety of your athletes. Our customizable Wall Pads and Post Pads provide comprehensive coverage for walls, poles, beams, and other obstructions, making your training environment safer for everyone. With our manufacturing processes now brought in-house at our headquarters in Oklahoma City, USA, we offer faster turnaround times, superior craftsmanship, and fully customizable solutions.

Heavy-Duty, Impact-Tested Protection

Our Wall and Post Pads are constructed from heavy-duty 14 oz vinyl, designed to endure intense training environments while providing superior protection. Tested to meet or exceed ASTM F355 safety standards, these pads deliver top-tier impact absorption, ensuring the safety of your athletes during every session.

Wall Pads: Customizable Protection for Any Wall

Century’s Wall Pads are fully customizable to fit any wall size or shape. Whether you need standard sizes such as 2’ x 4’ or 2’ x 6’, or custom-made pieces tailored to your facility’s specific dimensions, we’ve got you covered. You can also personalize your padding by adding your business or school logo, giving your facility a polished and branded look.

For facilities requiring quick and easy setup, we offer roll-out wall pads, which allow you to rapidly cover large areas without permanent installation. This versatile option is ideal for temporary setups or spaces that need frequent adjustments.

Post Pads: Tailored Versatility for Unique Spaces

Not every training facility is perfectly square. Our Post Pads are designed to wrap around support columns, beams, and other obstructions, ensuring maximum safety in even the most uniquely shaped spaces. Whether you need a permanent or semi-permanent solution, Century Mat Solutions will help you minimize potential hazards without compromising the functionality or appearance of your training area.

Introducing Our New Corner and Column Pads

In addition to our traditional wall and post padding, we’re excited to introduce two new options for corner and column protection:

• 2-Sided Corner Pads: Perfect for covering sharp or exposed corners, these 6” x 6” pads provide crucial protection in tight areas where walls meet at a 90-degree angle.

• 3-Sided Column Pads: Designed for columns and other freestanding obstructions, these 6” x 6” x 6” pads offer 360-degree protection, keeping athletes safe from potential impact hazards while preserving your facility’s open layout.

Made in the USA for Unmatched Quality

All of our Wall and Post Pads, including the new Corner and Column Pads, are proudly manufactured in-house at our headquarters in Oklahoma City, USA. By controlling our production processes, we ensure precise customization, exceptional quality, and shorter lead times, all while supporting American manufacturing. When you choose Century Mat Solutions, you’re choosing durability and reliability.

Custom Solutions Tailored to Your Facility

We make it easy to find the right fit for your space. Simply provide us with your measurements, and we’ll take care of the rest! Our expert team will ensure that your Wall Pads, Post Pads, and Corner Pads are not only optimized for safety but also seamlessly integrated into your facility’s design.
