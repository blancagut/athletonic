About this product

Description

Our patented wall-mounted folding Profile® Squat Rack changed the home gym game. Today, PRx is continuing to deliver fully functional and innovative space-saving gym equipment with the new Profile® PRO Folding Full Cage! Now you can experience all the same space-saving conveniences of the original Profile Racks, but with the added comfort, and security, that can only come with four posts. Easily transform any garage, home, or small commercial space into a multi-functioning gym while sacrificing just 8" of floor space when not in use.

The Folding Full Cage is designed to work on sloped floors, both parallel with, and perpendicular to the wall. Putting it away is quick and pain free. Simply fold the pull-up bar down and easily tuck both sides of the rack against the wall. The wall brackets are spring assisted which drastically reduces the weight required to lift your rack while moving. The spring assist also helps prevent the uprights from dragging on your floor while being put away or taken out. 

As with all Profile® PRO Racks, the Folding Full Cage comes with heavy-duty J-Cups, laser cut numbers, 5/8th" holes, 2" hole spacing on the sides and 1" spacing in the bench area. It's available in 9 custom colors, and is made in the USA.
