Men’s Original Mesh Lifestyle Shorts – 5”

Built for movement. Designed for comfort. These Men’s Original Mesh Lifestyle Shorts are the perfect blend of performance and everyday wear. Crafted from midweight, top shelf mesh, they deliver great coverage and feel, all while keeping you cool whether you’re training, working, or kicking back.
