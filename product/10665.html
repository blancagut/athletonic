Schiek Lifting Belt Sizing Guide

Finding the right fit is essential for optimal support and performance. Use this guide to choose the perfect size for your Schiek lifting belt:

Size Waist Measurement Waist in CM
XS 24"–28" 60–71 cm
S 27"–32" 68–81 cm
M 31"–36" 78–91 cm
L 35"–41" 88–104 cm
XL 40"–45" 101–114 cm
XXL 44"–50" 111–127 cm

How to Measure for Your Belt:

  1. Measure Around Your Navel:
    Use a measuring tape to measure your waist at the level of your navel, not your pant size.

  2. Choose the Right Size:

    • If your measurement falls between two sizes, we recommend choosing the smaller size for a snug, supportive fit.

  3. Keep It Tight but Comfortable:
    A well-fitted belt should provide firm support without restricting your range of motion or causing discomfort.

Pro Tips:

  • Avoid using pant sizes—they often differ from true waist measurements.
  • For heavy lifts, a tighter fit offers better core stability and support.
