Replenishes Phospocreatine Stores

To say you put your body under intense levels of stress would be an understatement. In fact, you beat your body up and push it to its limit during your training sessions. However, the body’s phosphocreatine stores are limited and can become depleted during prolonged or brutal workouts, leading to muscle fatigue and impaired performance. NutraBio RELOAD helps replenish phosphocreatine stores and can help improve muscle recovery post-workout by restoring energy levels and promoting tissue repair.
