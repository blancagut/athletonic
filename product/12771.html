About this product

Imagine a treadmill that is half-folded, has a small volume, is lightweight, sturdy, and quiet. Well, it's not just a dream. Denise Austin 2.0 by WalkingPad brings you all these amazing features and greater speed to 7.5MPH.

The fashion grey color of the previous version remains a classic choice, and now with Powder Blue and Taupe added to the palette, Denise Austin 2.0 offers a wider range of beautiful colors to match any home decor. The unique painting technology ensures a long service life and a high-end look that is both stylish and lively. No matter where you place it, Denise Austin 2.0 fits in perfectly.

  • Unique Fold Tech: Like other WalkingPad treadmills, Denise Austin also holds a half-fold feature, 180-degree fold lets you put it anywhere you want, is great for apartments and other limited spaces, and you can also quickly open it when used.
  • Comfortable Side Handrail:  Gone are the sharp corners, making it even more comfortable to hold. It offers double safety protection during your runs and can be folded away when not in use.
  • Professional Wide Belt:  Four layers professional runway design gives Denise Austin better shock and noise reduction capabilities, matched with EVA cushion layers, WalkingPad Denise Austin can protect your knee and ankle effectively, besides, it also enjoys great anti-wear and anti-slip technology.
  • The Aluminum Alloy Frame: ensures that the WalkingPad Denise Austin is lightweight but sturdy.
  • Clear Led Display on Handle: records kinds of data, like time, distance, speed, distance, and burned calories, one second you will see it clearly no matter under strong sunshine or dimming light.
  • Phone & Table Holder: offers big convenience to satisfy your environment when running.
