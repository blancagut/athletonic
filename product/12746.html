About this product

The Red and Infrared Light Therapy Lamp Panel with Stand from LumyHealth is a versatile and powerful therapy tool designed to enhance your wellness routine. Combining red (630nm) and near-infrared (850nm) light, this lamp promotes pain relief, muscle recovery, skin rejuvenation, and overall wellness. The adjustable stand allows you to position the lamp for targeted treatments, making it easy to direct light to specific areas of the body. Whether you're looking to reduce inflammation, alleviate joint pain, or improve your skin health, this lamp offers a non-invasive, drug-free solution. Ideal for both home and clinical use, it’s perfect for anyone seeking the benefits of red and infrared light therapy in a convenient, easy-to-use device.

Key Features:

  1. Dual Red and Infrared Light Therapy
    Combines 630nm red light and 850nm near-infrared light to provide a comprehensive treatment for pain relief, skin health, and overall wellness.

  2. Pain Relief and Inflammation Reduction
    The combination of red and infrared light helps reduce pain and inflammation, improving circulation and promoting faster recovery from muscle and joint soreness.

  3. Skin Rejuvenation and Anti-Aging
    Red light stimulates collagen production, reducing wrinkles, fine lines, and improving skin texture, making it ideal for those seeking youthful, radiant skin.

  4. Adjustable Stand for Targeted Treatment
    The built-in stand allows you to adjust the angle of the lamp, making it easy to target specific areas of your body for personalized therapy.

  5. Non-Invasive, Drug-Free Solution
    A safe and natural alternative to medications, this lamp offers a non-invasive way to promote healing, improve circulation, and enhance overall health.

  6. Easy to Use
    The lamp is user-friendly, with simple controls that make it easy to start therapy sessions. Just position the lamp, set the intensity, and enjoy therapeutic benefits.

  7. Energy-Efficient LED Technology
    The LED light technology is both energy-efficient and long-lasting, providing consistent results without high electricity costs.

  8. Durable and High-Quality Build
    Made with premium materials for durability and long-lasting performance, ensuring you get the best results from your therapy sessions.
