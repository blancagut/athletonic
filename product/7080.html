NutraBio LEG DAY not only provides your muscles with the highest quality BCAAs in the scientifically studied 2:1:1 ratio, but it also includes ALL of the essential amino acids (EAAs).

ELECTROLYTES & HYDRATION

LEG DAY helps take your hydration to the next level by including key minerals and electrolytes to replenish what was lost during your workout. Proper hydration is essential when it comes to workout performance. As little as a 2% reduction in body water can drastically reduce performance levels and hinder your results.

Every cell in your body needs water. For your muscles to function, water is required to help facilitate the electrical current that creates an impulse the muscle needs to be told to contract and relax. By managing hydration and electrolyte levels, the body is better able to transport key nutrients (such as amino acids) out to the working muscles, which can help reduce fatigue, improve endurance, and enhance recovery.

Something else to consider is that maintaining hydration can help improve protein synthesis, which is essential in building lean muscle mass. Additionally, you need adequate hydration levels to achieve the coveted pump during workouts and push fluid into the muscles. Otherwise, you risk looking flat, and your muscularity can appear drastically reduced.
