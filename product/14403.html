Goldenrod Extract 10:1 (Solidago decurrens Lour) (Whole Plant)

Goldenrod (Solidago virgaurea) is a versatile herb that has been used for centuries in traditional medicine to support kidney and bladder health. Most notably used for its diuretic, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, Goldenrod can be a suitable choice for those looking to maintain a healthy urinary system and manage conditions such as urinary tract infections (UTIs) and kidney stones.

One of the primary benefits of Goldenrod is its diuretic effect, which promotes healthy urine flow and can help flush out toxins and other waste products from the kidneys. This increased urine output can be particularly beneficial for preventing the formation of kidney stones by reducing the concentration of minerals and salts in the urine that can lead to stone formation.

In addition to its diuretic properties, Goldenrod is known for its anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial effects, which can help manage and prevent urinary tract infections (UTIs). UTIs are often caused by bacteria that find a home within the urinary tract, leading to discomfort, pain, and frequent urination. Goldenrod contains compounds such as saponins and flavonoids, which have been shown to inhibit the growth of harmful bacteria and reduce inflammation in the urinary tract. Several studies have demonstrated that Goldenrod extract exhibits strong antimicrobial activity against common UTI-causing bacteria, such as E. coli, and other harmful bacteria. By combating bacterial growth and soothing inflammation, Goldenrod can aid in the alleviation of UTI symptoms and support the body's natural defenses against infection.

Goldenrod can also offer support for overall bladder health, particularly in managing conditions like overactive bladder (OAB) and bladder inflammation. Again, its anti-inflammatory properties can help reduce irritation in the bladder, while its diuretic action can ensure that the bladder is regularly flushed, preventing the buildup of irritants that can trigger OAB symptoms. Goldenrod’s ability to strengthen and tone the urinary tract muscles can contribute to better bladder control, reducing the frequency and urgency of urination.

PaCran® Cranberry Extract

Cranberry (Vaccinium macrocarpon), a rich and distinctive source of flavonoids and phenolic acids, has been often utilized to protect against urinary tract infections (UTIs), the second most common type of infection worldwide. These infections, as we have discovered above, are often triggered from the exposure of unwanted bacterium, most notably E. coli, and can lead to significant issues that not only affect internal organs, but also can negatively affect overall quality of life. Cranberry’s being rich is flavonoids, particularly proanthocyanidins, anthocyanidins, and flavanols, along with other phenolic acids and benzoates, provide some key mechanisms of action for their protective effects. These flavonoids have been shown to act as antiadhesive agents in preventing/inhibiting the adherence of pathogens to uroepithelial cell receptors, which appear to be a major component in the pathogenesis of UTIs.

PaCran® is a clinically studied cranberry extract specifically designed to support overall kidney and bladder health. Unlike other traditional cranberry products, PaCran® combines the full spectrum of cranberry's beneficial compounds mentioned above, which are known for their ability to promote healthy urinary tract function and aid in the prevention of infection. One study looking at the effects of cranberry ingestion on urinary health demonstrated that women who supplemented with a cranberry extract rich in PACs experienced a significant reduction in the recurrence of UTIs. This data suggests that PaCran® can be an effective preventive measure for those prone to recurrent UTIs.

In addition to its role in preventing infections, PaCran® also supports overall bladder health by reducing inflammation and promoting regular urinary flow. The anti-inflammatory properties of cranberry help soothe the urinary tract, reducing irritation and discomfort that can be caused by infections or other urinary conditions. By promoting healthy bladder function, PaCran® can help to maintain a comfortable and efficient urinary flow, reducing the frequency and urgency of urination often associated with bladder irritation or overactive bladder (OAB).

PaCran® also can offer benefits for kidney health. The potent antioxidant properties of PaCran® can help protect the kidneys from high amounts of oxidative stress, which can lead to cellular damage and impair kidney function over time. By neutralizing free radicals, PaCran® can support the kidneys' ability to efficiently filter and eliminate toxins, contributing to long-term kidney health.

Backed by several human clinical studies, PaCran® is a natural and well-tolerated supplement, making it a safe option for long-term use in maintaining urinary health. It offers a more convenient and concentrated form of cranberry's benefits without the high sugar content often found in cranberry juice, making it an ideal choice for individuals looking to manage their urinary health naturally and effectively.

More recently, stinging nettle has been utilized for the purpose of maintaining normal urinary flow and contributing to a healthy, optimal functioning prostate. Stinging nettle has also shown effectiveness when used in combination with other herbs, like saw palmetto (included above). Grape Seed Extract (Vitis vinifera) (Seed) (Std. to 95% Proanthocyanidins)

Grape seed extract, a powerful, antioxidant rich compound derived from the seeds of grapes, often utilized for it benefits for cardiovascular health, also has been shown to offer significant benefit for the kidneys and bladder. Primarily through its ability to combat oxidative stress, reduce inflammation, and promote overall urinary tract function, grape seed extract can be a beneficial compound to support these areas.

Grape seed extracts primary benefits for kidney health lies in its potent antioxidant properties. The kidneys are constantly exposed to oxidative stress as they filter toxins and waste products from the blood. Over time, this exposure can lead to cellular damage, contributing to the development of chronic kidney disease (CKD) and other kidney-related issues. The proanthocyanidins found in grape seed extract have been shown to neutralize free radicals and protect kidney cells from excessive oxidative damage.

In addition to its antioxidant effects, grape seed extract also has inflammatory mitigating properties that benefit both the kidneys and bladder. Chronic inflammation is a common factor in many urinary tract issues, including interstitial cystitis (IC) and other forms of bladder irritation. Grape seed extract has been shown to reduce inflammation by inhibiting the production of pro-inflammatory cytokines and enzymes, such as COX-2.

Grape seed extract may also play a role in protecting against urinary tract infections (UTIs). The antimicrobial properties of grape seed extract, combined with its ability to strengthen the bladder lining, can help create an environment that is less favorable for bacterial growth.

Astragalus Extract (Astragalus membranaceous) (Root) (Std. to 50% Polysaccharides)

Astragalus extract (Astragalus membranaceous) has been shown to offer powerful benefits for overall urinary system health. Polysaccharides, the active compounds in Astragalus, are known for their immune-boosting, anti-inflammatory, and potent antioxidant properties.

One of the primary benefits of Astragalus extract for kidney health is its ability to protect against renal damage and support kidney function. The polysaccharides contained within Astragalus have been shown to reduce proteinuria, which is the presence of excess protein in the urine. This can be a common sign of kidney damage, particularly in conditions like chronic kidney disease (CKD).

The antioxidant properties of Astragalus polysaccharides can further enhance kidney protection by combating oxidative stress, which is a major contributor to kidney damage.
