Safety and Side Effects

Tribulus terrestris has become a popular dietary supplement for its supposed health benefits, especially for sexual function, erectile dysfunction, and testosterone levels. While many users take tribulus terrestris extract to improve sexual dysfunction, low libido, and even athletic performance, we need to consider the safety and side effects of tribulus terrestris supplementation.

Most clinical trials and animal studies show that tribulus terrestris is generally safe when taken at recommended doses. However, some people may experience mild side effects like stomach cramps or gastrointestinal discomfort, especially with higher doses. There have been rare reports of more serious side effects, like kidney damage and liver problems, in animal studies. This highlights the need for more research to fully understand the long-term safety of Tribulus terrestris, especially for those who use it as a testosterone booster or to boost testosterone levels.

The benefits of tribulus terrestris are often attributed to its steroidal saponins, which may increase testosterone, inhibit oxidative stress, and improve body composition. In traditional Chinese medicine and Ayurvedic medicine, tribulus terrestris has been used for centuries to treat high blood pressure, kidney stones, and low libido. The aqueous extract of this aphrodisiac herb is believed to support sexual health and act as a natural testosterone booster. 

Athletes should also be aware of the doping control analysis issues. There have been concerns about tribulus terrestris supplements being contaminated with banned substances, which can result in positive drug tests. Some reports also link tribulus terrestris supplementation to increased risk of blood clots and cardiovascular problems, although these are rare. Given the mixed results and the side effects, consult with a doctor before taking tribulus terrestris. Especially if you have underlying medical conditions, take prescription meds, or use tribulus terrestris as a testosterone booster or to boost athletic performance. As with any dietary supplement, be cautious and informed to get the most benefits with minimal risks.
