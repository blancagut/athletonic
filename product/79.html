Introducing Gorilla Mind Creatine Gummies.

A daily essential, made easy (and actually enjoyable).

Creatine monohydrate is one of the most researched performance supplements available. Studies show it can augment strength, power output, lean mass, recovery and may support aspects of cognitive performance [R, R, R, R, R].

But despite its effectiveness, many people struggle to take creatine consistently due to gritty powders, bland flavors, and inconsistent product quality, especially in gummy formats, where maintaining label-accurate dosing is difficult.

That’s why we built these differently.

Gorilla Mind Creatine Gummies deliver a full 5 g per serving in a convenient, great-tasting gummy: no mixing, no chalky texture, no hassle.

And we don’t cut corners on inputs: we use Creapure® creatine monohydrate, a gold-standard creatine made in Germany and recognized for exceptional purity and consistency [R]. Our gummies are third-party tested and formulated with a calculated overage to account for natural degradation in gummy formats, so you get at least 5 g per serving throughout shelf life.

Daily creatine just got a lot easier.
