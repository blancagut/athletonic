About this product

The ultimate gym and travel companion! 

Designed for the athlete on the move, this roller bag combines durability, capacity, and convenience in one premium package. Made of high-quality tarpaulin material, it’s tough, water-resistant, and built to withstand the demands of daily training or international travel.

Featuring a spacious main compartment with a large top opening, this bag offers plenty of room for your gloves, apparel, footwear, and protective gear. Multiple zippered pockets provide smart organization, while reinforced handles and smooth-rolling wheels ensure effortless transport wherever you go. Also, to keep your wet or dirty gear separate, this bag features a waterproof dry bag — perfect for training sessions, competitions, or weekend trips.

Whether you’re heading to the gym, traveling to an event, or packing for fight week, this roller bag has you covered.
