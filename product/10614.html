  4. One-Way Velcro Closure System 🔒 Provides an exact, secure fit every time, preventing slippage or loosening during use.

  5. Durable USA-Made Construction 🇺🇸 Proudly crafted in the USA with premium materials for long-lasting performance and reliability.

  6. 2-Year Warranty 🛡️ Backed by a 2-year warranty, giving you peace of mind about the belt’s durability and quality.

Why Choose the Schiek Model 4004 with Suspenders?

  • Enhanced Stability: The combination of suspenders and the contoured belt ensures unmatched support and stability.
  • Maximum Comfort: Designed with your anatomy in mind, it offers a snug, comfortable fit without sacrificing range of motion.

Support Belt Sizing Guide:

To ensure the perfect fit, measure your waist at the navel (not your pants size) and refer to the chart below:

Size Waist Measurement (inches)
Small (S) 27" - 32"
Medium (M) 31" - 36"
Large (L) 35" - 41"
X-Large (XL) 40" - 45"
2X-Large (2XL) 44" - 50"
3X-Large (3XL) 49" - 55"

Pro Tip: If you’re between sizes, opt for the larger size for a more comfortable fit.
