Frequently Asked Questions

What Is NOVOS Age?

NOVOS Age is an advanced biological aging test that provides insights into your rate of aging, biological age, organ ages, and telomere length. It combines scientific rigor with ease of use to help you manage your health and longevity effectively.

How does the NOVOS Age test work?

Simply collect a blood sample at home using the provided kit, then send it back to our labs using the prepaid envelope. Our experts analyze your sample using the latest in biotechnology, and you’ll receive a detailed report about your biological aging.

What makes NOVOS Age different from other aging tests?

NOVOS Age is the only test that uses both the DunedinPACE and Organ Age algorithms to provide a comprehensive view of your aging rate and biological age both overall and across different organs. Over 45 scientific studies support it and offer actionable insights for personalized health strategies.

Can I use NOVOS Age to improve my health?

Absolutely! The detailed report you receive informs you about your current aging status and includes customized advice on lifestyle changes that can improve your health and slow down aging.

Is the NOVOS Age test easy to use?

Yes, it is designed for easy at-home use. The kit includes all necessary materials for blood collection and a prepaid envelope for easy sample return.

How often should I take the NOVOS Age test?

We recommend retesting annually to monitor how lifestyle changes impact biological aging and track improvements over time.

What support is available after I receive my NOVOS Age results?

Alongside your results, you’ll gain access to NOVOS Life, an app that provides ongoing personalized recommendations and insights to help you enhance your longevity based on your test results.

How Long Will It Take To See My Results?

You’ll receive an email when the lab receives your sample. Your results will be ready 2-4 weeks after that date.

Once your results are ready, you will be able to access them by signing into your secure online account.

What is your return policy?

For NOVOS Age, due to the nature of the product, all sales are final. Currently, we only ship in the United States and Canada. Please be sure that your shipping address is accurate before submitting your order.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, in addition to the United States, we ship NOVOS Age to Canada. You can also purchase our other products like NOVOS Core and NOVOS Boost in many other non-US countries.

Is it really possible to slow or reverse aging?

Yes. While you can’t change the number of birthdays that have passed (chronological age), science has proven it is possible to slow down or accelerate your biological age based upon lifestyle changes.
