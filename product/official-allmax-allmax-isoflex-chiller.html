Product overview

Performance starts with the right formula.

Isoflex® Chiller delivers the same ultra-premium whey protein isolate you trust — now in a light, refreshing, juice-style drink that tastes like an ice-cold summer beverage. With 27 g of protein per serving (90% pure protein), zero sugar, zero fat, low carbohydrate content, over 98% lactose-free formula, and instant mixability, Isoflex® Chiller gives you clean, fast-digesting protein without heaviness or creaminess. Just mix with ice-cold water, pour over ice, and enjoy a refreshing, crystal-clear protein drink anytime you need a protein boost. Made in a cGMP-certified, air-sealed manufacturing facility and using cutting-edge isolate technology, Isoflex® Chiller delivers exceptional purity, superior absorption, and incredible taste—perfect for athletes, active individuals, and anyone who wants a lighter, more refreshing protein experience. Why Choose Isoflex Chiller? Isoflex® Chiller is the evolution of whey protein isolate — combining ultra-clean protein with a crystal-clear, refreshing drink experience. It’s designed for athletes who want ultra-high purity and fast absorption without the creaminess. With 27 g of pure protein per serving, zero sugar, and instant mixability, Isoflex® Chiller supports recovery, lean muscle growth, and daily protein needs in the most enjoyable, refreshing format possible. Ultra-Pure Whey Protein Isolate (WPI90) Isoflex® Chiller contains 90% pure whey protein isolate, ensuring maximum bioavailability. This ultra-clean formula provides a rapid influx of amino acids to support muscle repair, post-workout recovery, and lean muscle development. Crystal-Clear, Light, and Refreshing Unlike heavy, milky shakes, Isoflex® Chiller mixes into a clear, juice-like beverage that is incredibly refreshing—especially post-workout.
