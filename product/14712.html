About this product

Codeage Liposomal NRHM+ features Nicotinamide Riboside Hydrogen Malate (NRH) paired with 98% Trans-Resveratrol, D-Ribose, and Quercetin. This advanced supplement is further enhanced with Vitamin C, Magnesium, and Zinc. A refined blend centered on premium ingredients, with liposomal delivery.

  • Codeage Liposomal NRHM+ features 300 mg of Nicotinamide Riboside Hydrogen Malate (NRH) per serving in a convenient capsule format. This specialized compound is a reduced form related to nicotinamide riboside (NR), provided in a defined salt format for daily use.
  • This NRH botanical supplement includes 98% standardized Trans-Resveratrol from Japanese Knotweed with Quercetin Dihydrate and D-Ribose. These premium ingredients are selected to complement the NRH blend, offering a multi-faceted approach to your wellness routine.
  • Codeage Liposomal NRHM+ also includes vitamin C, magnesium (as citrate and oxide), and zinc, offering a well-rounded nutrient profile in each daily serving.
  • This Nicotinamide Riboside supplement in capsule form provides 60 servings for a 2-month supply and features liposomal delivery with phospholipids derived from non-GMO sunflower lecithin.
  • Codeage Liposomal NRHM+ is vegan, non-GMO, and free from dairy, soy, and gluten. This NRH and resveratrol supplement is manufactured in the USA with global ingredients in a cGMP-certified facility for quality and purity.
