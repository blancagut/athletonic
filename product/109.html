Introducing the most aggressive high-stim pre-workout we've ever released: Gorilla Mode Lightning

This innovative formula was designed to push CNS, dopaminergic, and adrenergic signaling, harder than any product we've ever created [R, R, R].

It's also packed with potent concentrations of vasodilating, volumizing, and hydrating actives to ensure you are not sacrificing pump for energy [R, R].

High-stim pre-workouts are notorious for causing significant vasoconstriction, which can reduce muscular blood flow and hinder pumps.
