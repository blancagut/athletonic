Scientific Breakdown

Recovery depends on positive net protein balance: slowing muscle protein breakdown while increasing synthesis. After training, protein supplements provide the amino acids your body needs to tip this balance in your favor.

But not all proteins are equal. Many products are filled with low-quality blends and fillers. Core PRO is different. Our cold-processed whey concentrate, cross-flow micro filtered whey isolate, and milk protein isolate deliver:

  • A complete amino acid profile

  • Superior nitrogen retention

  • Increased lean muscle impact

  • Strong satiety (feeling of fullness)

And unlike most brands, we’re fully transparent. Our label discloses exact yields and the full amino acid profile. Each serving provides 2.6g of leucine, the proven dose to maximize muscle protein synthesis.

The Core PRO Protein Sources

Cold-Processed Whey Concentrate: Preserves amino acid structure and immune-boosting benefits while supplying antioxidants like glutathione. A strong base for recovery nutrition.

Cross-Flow Whey Isolate: Extremely clean, with minimal fat and lactose, making it highly digestible and rapidly absorbed without harsh chemicals.

Milk Protein Isolate: Over 90% protein by weight, containing both casein and whey in their natural ratios. Low fat, low lactose, and high in amino acids for sustained recovery.

This combination provides immediate recovery support and long-lasting amino acid delivery until your next meal.

Added Support Ingredients

DigeSEB Digestive Enzyme Blend: Many brands overlook enzymes, but they are crucial for breaking down carbs, fats, and proteins. Core PRO includes amylases, proteases, lactases, and cellulases to ensure complete digestion and absorption. This makes your protein more effective both pre- and post-workout.

Coconut Oil Creamer (MCTs): MCTs bypass gastric uptake, go directly to the liver, and are rapidly used for energy. Research shows they increase resting energy expenditure by up to 65 percent while reducing fat storage, supporting both performance and body composition.
