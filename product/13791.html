When Should I Take Animal Pak Vitamins?

Universal Nutrition recommends taking 1 pak thirty minutes after breakfast with plenty of water. For competitions and athletes undertaking intense training, take 2 paks daily, separated by 4-12 hours.

Animal Pak only contains 40 calories per serving, that's the equivalent to a couple Tic Tac's. Animal Pak will not cause you to gain weight unless you're consuming significant amounts of protein or calories with it, with the intention of gaining weight and putting on muscle. 

Does Animal Pak Contain Creatine?

Animal Pak is an advanced multivitamin, it does not contain creatine. 

Will Universal Animal Pak 44 Increase My Testosterone?

Animal Pak is not itself a Test Booster, however it is packed full of nutrients to help users stay healthy, stay in the gym, and increase size and strength, so in a roundabout way it potentially could help you boost your testosterone. 

Does Animal Pak Contain Stimulants?

No Animal Pak 44 does not contain caffeine, synephrine, or any other stimulants. 

Is Animal Pak a Steroid?

Animal Pak is not a steroid, it's actually a steroid free supplement which is designed to support anabolic muscle growth. It contains no steroids and no steroid derivatives.  For those looking to increase Testosterone, Size, and Strength, checkout Animal Stack. 

Why Does Animal Pak Make My Urine Funny Colors?

Animal Pak contains Riboflavin, this vitamin is naturally yellow and becomes neon like when it comes into contact with UV light. Because Riboflavin and other B Vitamins are water soluble, the human body gets rid of excess through your urine. 

Can I Crush Animal Pak Pills?

No, you should not crush Animal Pak pills. If you have a difficult time swallowing pills take them one at a time. 

Do Bodybuilders & Athletes Need Multivitamins?

It's critical athletes and bodybuilders take a multivitamin. Vitamins, Minerals, and Nutrients are necessary for thousands of metabolic processes. Bodybuilders, Athletes, and anyone leading an active lifestyle need even more nutrients than your average office worker, or person who isn't active. 

How to Take Animal Pak Pills?

Take 1-2 packs with breakfast or any meal of the day. Drink plenty of water when taking your Animal Pak.
