Key Benefits at a Glance: 1,250 mg pharmaceutical-grade creatine monohydrate per gummy Sugar-free, zero fillers, no mixing required Low-temperature gummy technology : no pectin, no heat degradation Lab tested every lot in a cGMP-registered facility Cognitive support : Creatine supports cellular energy production in the brain Available in: Tropical Fruit Punch and Raspberry Lemonade flavors (90 gummies per bottle) Low-Temperature Gummy Technology: Why It Matters Most creatine gummies use pectin-based gelling agents that require high heat during processing, which can degrade creatine before it reaches you. ALLMAX Creatine Gummies use a proprietary, pectin-free gelling agent processed at low temperatures to preserve full creatine potency in every gummy. Not all creatine gummies are manufactured the same way, and the production process directly affects potency. Most creatine gummies on the market use pectin-based gelling agents that require processing temperatures of approximately 104°C (219°F) to form. Creatine, however, can begin to degrade at temperatures as low as 97°C (207°F), meaning standard high-heat gummy manufacturing may compromise creatine integrity before the product ever reaches you. What Makes Allmax Creatine Gummies Different ALLMAX Creatine Gummies use a proprietary, pectin-free gelling system derived from Eucheuma seaweed, processed at approximately 40°C (104°F). This low-temperature manufacturing approach is specifically designed to maintain creatine stability throughout production. The result: Supports creatine integrity and potency through the manufacturing process Avoids the high-heat processing used in typical pectin-based gummies Delivers a more reliable dose of creatine per gummy The ISSN position stand on creatine (2017) notes that creatine monohydrate remains the most extensively researched and effective form of creatine available, making delivery format and manufacturing quality critical factors when choosing a creatine product. Are Creatine Gummies as Effective as Powder? Creatine gummies and creatine powder contain the same active ingredient: creatine monohydrate. Research confirms that creatine monohydrate supports strength, muscle recovery, and training performance when taken consistently at an adequate daily dose.
