Why Choose CGT-MAX?

Muscle Growth

A powerful combination of supplements that could unlock your body’s potential for muscle growth is none other than creatine, glutamine, and taurine. It has been found that these highly researched ingredients work together synergistically, enhancing each other’s effects and leading to bigger, stronger muscles. The two forms of creatine in CGT-MAX help increase ATP production and energy as well as power output during workouts, while glutamine supports muscle recovery and growth. Taurine, a potent amino acid, has been added and shown to improve muscle endurance and reduce fatigue. Together, these ingredients create a dynamic trifecta that can help take your muscle-building game to the next level.

Strength & Power

Imagine having the explosive strength and power to set PRs and blast through plateaus in your training. It’s not just a dream or wishful thinking — with the matrix of multiple forms of creatine, glutamine, and taurine used in CGT-MAX, they have been shown to work together to enhance strength, boost power output, reduce muscle fatigue, and amplify explosiveness during training. After you’ve annihilated your workout, CGT-MAX continues to work by improving muscle recovery, which is essential for building strength.

Muscle Recovery

When it comes to building muscle and improving fitness, recovery is just as important as the workout itself. CGT-MAX helps accelerate muscle recovery and reduce soreness after intense workouts. The powerful blend of creatine, glutamine, and taurine can help enhance muscle recovery and tissue repair while also reducing overall muscle damage. Not only can better muscle recovery help you during your workouts, but it can also help you maximize your muscle growth.
