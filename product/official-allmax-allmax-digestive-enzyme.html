ALLMAX Digestive Enzymes: Full-Spectrum Multi-Enzyme Formula for Digestion & Nutrient Absorption ALLMAX Digestive Enzymes is a comprehensive multi-enzyme formula delivering 11 highly active enzymes across three targeted digestion complexes designed to support protein breakdown, fat digestion, carbohydrate metabolism, and reduce occasional digestive discomfort. Each capsule provides a full-spectrum blend of enzymes that remain active across a range of pH levels, making it suitable for athletes, active individuals, and anyone on a high-protein or specialized nutrition plan. Key Benefits at a Glance: Supports protein digestion across multiple pH levels with four distinct protease forms Helps reduce occasional gas, bloating, and indigestion associated with large or high-protein meals Supports fat digestion and dairy tolerance via lactase and lipase in the Lacto-Lipid Complex Supports breakdown of plant-based foods, including carbohydrates, fruits, and vegetables Vegan, Kosher Certified, manufactured in a cGMP Registered Facility, and Lab Tested Every Lot 90 servings per container Multi-pH Protein Digestion Complex The Protein Digestion Complex features four distinct protease forms: Protease SP (30,000 HUT), Bromelain (50,000 PU), Protease (6,000 HUT), and High pH Protease (50 SAPU), each active in a different pH range. This layered approach supports protein digestion from the stomach through the intestines, helping to make amino acids available for muscle recovery and growth. Research published in the Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition suggests that co-ingesting proteolytic enzymes with protein sources may increase blood amino acid levels and improve the bioavailability of plant proteins. Lacto-Lipid Digestion Complex The Lacto-Lipid Complex delivers Lactase (300 ALU) and Lipase (225 FIP/150 LU) to support fat digestion and lactose breakdown. This complex is particularly relevant for individuals who consume dairy-based proteins, such as ALLMAX AllWhey Gold , ALLMAX AllWhey Classic , or Hexapro , and who may experience occasional digestive discomfort after dairy-containing meals. Phyto Digestion Complex The Phyto Digestion Complex provides Amylase (10,000 DU), Cellulase (2,000 CU), Glucoamylase (50 AGU), Hemicellulase (50 HCU), and Pectinase (7.5 endo-PGU) to support the breakdown of carbohydrates, plant fibers, and starches. This complex is especially beneficial for those on plant-based diets, as plant proteins are often less bioavailable and harder to digest. Why Enzyme Supplementation Matters When foods are cooked, processed, baked, or microwaved, they lose their naturally occurring enzymes. The body then takes on the additional burden of producing enzymes, which can strain the pancreas and reduce overall digestive efficiency. As enzyme production naturally declines with age, supplementation can help support optimal digestion and nutrient availability, particularly for individuals who consume enzyme-depleted foods that make up a significant portion of modern diets.
