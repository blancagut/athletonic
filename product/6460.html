What ingredients are in NOVOS Vital?

NOVOS Vital contains seven targeted ingredients, each selected to support the healthy function of key organs. The formula includes:

  • Trehalose – supports brain and neuronal health, protein homeostasis and cellular balance
  • Acetic Acid (from Apple Cider Vinegar) – supports metabolism, memory, concentration and healthy cholesterol levels
  • Nattokinase – supports healthy brain aging, cardiovascular function and blood glucose balance
  • Lutein – supports nighttime vision, healthy eye aging and cognitive function
  • Zeaxanthin – supports retina health, healthy eye aging and brain health
  • Rutin – supports balanced liver function, cellular renewal, strength and mobility
  • Inulin – supports beneficial gut bacteria and metabolic health

Vital is formulated as a vegan, gluten-free longevity gummy, free from major allergens and crafted for daily support of brain, heart, eye and gut health.

How does NOVOS Vital support organ health?

NOVOS Vital is a longevity gummy formulated with nutrients that support healthy cellular, metabolic and organ-health pathways including the:

  • Brain
  • Heart
  • Eyes
  • Gut

Each ingredient is part of a blend scientifically proven in research to support these key areas of long-term health. Together, they help maintain healthy organ function and overall wellbeing as part of a proactive longevity routine.

How should I take NOVOS Vital?

Take four gummies daily, ideally with food. Daily use helps maintain the benefits of this longevity-focused formula.

Can I take NOVOS Vital with other supplements or medications?

Vital is compatible with most supplements and can be included alongside NOVOS Core. There are no known contraindications with medications, but speak with your doctor before starting any new supplement.

Is NOVOS Vital suitable for my diet?

NOVOS Vital is vegan-friendly, gluten-free, and free from major allergens, making it suitable for a wide range of dietary preferences.

Are there any side effects?

Most users tolerate Vital well. If you are sensitive to ingredients such as nattokinase, start slowly and increase over several days. Allergic reactions to natto-derived ingredients are rare but documented; discontinue use and consult a healthcare professional if symptoms occur.

I have a sensitive stomach. How should I consume NOVOS Vital?

If you have a sensitive stomach, you can:

  • Begin with one gummy per day for 3–7 days, then increase gradually.
  • Take Vital after meals and split the daily serving across the day if needed.
  • If you also take NOVOS Core, separate them by at least two hours.
What makes NOVOS Vital different from NOVOS Core?

While NOVOS Core is focused on addressing the 12 root causes of aging, NOVOS Vital is specifically formulated to support the health and functionality of essential organs with targeted nutrients.

Is NOVOS Vital safe during pregnancy or breastfeeding?

NOVOS recommends avoiding Vital during pregnancy or breastfeeding as a precaution.

How long should I take NOVOS Vital for?

Vital is intended for ongoing daily use. Because organ and brain health change gradually over time, consistent long-term use provides the strongest support.

Can I take NOVOS Vital with NOVOS Core?

Yes. Vital and Core complement each other. If you have a sensitive stomach, take them at least two hours apart.

Can I travel with NOVOS Vital?

Yes. Vital does not require refrigeration and can be stored at room temperature. Gummies can be placed in a small travel container for convenience.

What does NOVOS Vital taste like?

NOVOS Vital has a refreshing tropical flavor with no unhealthy sugars. Each longevity gummy is lightly sweetened, high in prebiotic fiber, and designed to be enjoyable to take daily as part of your long-term wellness routine.

What are the main benefits of NOVOS Vital?

Vital is designed to support healthy organ function, cognitive performance, metabolic balance and overall vitality. Consistent daily use helps maintain long-term health as part of a proactive longevity routine.
