Why is it Called Black Ant?

Black ant extract (often listed as Polyrhachis vicina or “Chinese black ant”) is a powdered extract made from a species of black ant traditionally used in Chinese medicine. In supplements—especially pre-workouts—it’s included for a mix of energy, endurance, and hormonal-support claims.

  • Supports Energy & Less Fatigue
  • Increases Blood Flow and Pumps
  • Can Support Hormonal Increases
