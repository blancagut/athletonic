Schiek Lifting Belt Sizing Guide

Finding the right fit is crucial for optimal performance and support. Use this guide to choose the perfect size for your Schiek CF3006 Carbon Fiber Lifting Belt.

Size Waist Measurement Waist in CM
XS 22"–27" 56–69 cm
S 27"–32" 69–81 cm
M 31"–36" 78–91 cm
L 35"–41" 88–104 cm
XL 40"–45" 101–114 cm
XXL 44"–50" 111–127 cm

How to Measure for Your Belt:

  1. Measure Around Your Navel: Use a measuring tape to measure your waist at the level of your navel—not your pant size.

  2. Choose the Right Size: If your measurement falls between two sizes, we recommend selecting the smaller size for a snug, supportive fit.

  3. Keep It Tight but Comfortable: A well-fitted belt should provide firm support without restricting movement or causing discomfort.

Pro Tips:

  • Avoid using pant sizes—they often differ from true waist measurements.
  • For heavy lifts, a tighter fit provides better core stability and support.
