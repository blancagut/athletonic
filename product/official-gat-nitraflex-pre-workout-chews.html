Performance starts with the right formula.

A champion isn’t afraid to put in the work and try different things. They tend to bite off more than they can chew, but are willing to be uncomfortable because that’s where growth happens. Packed with proven, high-impact ingredients, Nitraflex® Pre-Workout Chews give you the energy and focus necessary to attack every rep, every set, every workout. No shaker. No compromise. Maximize Strength & Enhance Pumps Improve Endurance, Performance, & Energy Fast-Acting Energy with Less Than 1g of Sugar Key Ingredients L-Citrulline To support your peak performance, L-citrulline can help increase nitric oxide production, improve blood flow, and enhance muscle pumps, allowing you to train harder and push through intense workouts. Beta-Alanine Beta-alanine helps buffer lactic acid buildup in your muscles during high-intensity training, delaying muscle fatigue and increasing endurance so you can maintain your performance throughout your entire workout. Betaine Anhydrous A proven performance ingredient that supports increased power output, strength, and muscular endurance, helping you maximize force production during brutal training sessions. Caffeine Anhydrous Caffeine anhydrous is a common stimulant that delivers fast-acting energy, increased alertness, and enhanced endurance, helping you stay locked in and perform at a high level from the first rep to the last. L-Theanine L-theanine works alongside caffeine to help promote smooth, focused energy while helping reduce annoying jitters and crashes, allowing for improved mental clarity and sustained performance until your very last rep. Huperzine A Huperzine A supports cognitive function, focus, and the coveted mind-muscle connection by maintaining optimal neurotransmitter activity, helping you get and stay dialed in and mentally sharp throughout your workout. HOW TO USE: As a dietary supplement fully chew 1 serving (4 chewable tablets) with 8-10 fl. oz. of water 30 minutes prior to training. For best results, chew thoroughly before swallowing. Maintain an adequate state of hydration during use. Read the entire label before use and follow directions provided. DO NOT EXCEED 4 CHEWS WITHIN ANY 24-HOUR PERIOD. WHAT TO AVOID: This product is strictly intended for use by healthy persons 18 years of age or older. Each serving (4 chews) contains 200 mg of caffeine. Do not combine with other caffeine or stimulant-containing products. Do not use if you are pregnant, nursing or trying to become pregnant. Do not use if you are currently unaware of your health status or if you have a medical condition including but not limited to heart, liver, kidney, or thyroid disease, psychiatric or epileptic disorders, high or low blood pressure, cardiac arrhythmia, recurrent headaches, enlarged prostate or glaucoma. Do not use if you are taking prescription or non-prescription medication of any kind including but not limited to MAO inhibitors, anti-depressants, aspirin or non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs. Do not use if you are prone to overheating or dehydration. After 8 weeks of use, take 1 week off before resuming use. Discontinue at least 2 weeks prior to surgery or if you experience rapid heartbeat, dizziness, severe headache or shortness of breath. KEEP OUT OF REACH OF CHILDREN.
