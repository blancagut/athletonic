Build Limitless® 6-Post Power Rack

The biggest, and baddest. You're looking to go all out. You want everything. All the attachments. All the storage. You want the Build Limitless™ 6-Post Power Rack.

All Build Limitless Racks have 1" hole spacing on the uprights from 22" to 46". This allows for precise J-cup and spotter arm placement where it really matters. Laser-cut numbers on the front and back of the uprights provide easy reference when moving attachments. The hole spacing on the side of each upright is in increments of 2" and allows for more options and adjustability for all kinds of attachments. The Build Limitless line is also available in nine colors if you want to go for that extra pop!

Made in the USA.

We recommend anchoring the rack to the floor (anchoring hardware not included).

All Build Limitless® Racks Ship in 3-5 business days. Unless otherwise noted on your order confirmation.
