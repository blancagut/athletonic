Product overview

Performance starts with the right formula.

How ALLMAX Aminocore BCAA Supplement Supports Muscle Growth and Recovery Allmax Aminocore is a BCAA supplement powder delivering 8.18 g of branched-chain amino acids per serving in a clinically informed 9:6:5 (45:30:25) ratio. Each scoop provides 3,681 mg of L-Leucine, 2,454 mg L-Valine, and 2,045 mg L-Isoleucine, formulated to activate the mTOR signaling pathway, which is responsible for muscle protein synthesis. Unlike standard amino acid supplements that pad formulas with non-BCAA fillers, Aminocore contains zero sugar, zero fillers, and zero non-BCAA amino acids. It is also completely caffeine-free , making it suitable for evening training sessions or stacking with stimulant-based pre-workouts without exceeding daily caffeine thresholds. Why the 9:6:5 (45:30:25) BCAA Ratio Matters Aminocore uses a leucine-dominant 9:6:5 (45:30:25) BCAA ratio rather than the standard 2:1:1 (50:25:25) found in most BCAA supplements. Research published in the American Journal of Physiology – Cell Physiology demonstrates that leucine independently activates the mTOR signaling cascade, the master regulator of muscle protein synthesis, but that isoleucine and valine are required to sustain that response over time. The 9:6:5 (45:30:25) ratio optimizes leucine's anabolic signal while providing higher concentrations of valine and isoleucine than standard formulas, supporting sustained recovery and reduced muscle fatigue. B-Vitamin Complex for BCAA Metabolism Aminocore includes a targeted B-vitamin complex (B3, B6, Folate, B12) to support amino acid metabolism and energy production. Vitamin B6, in particular, serves as a coenzyme in branched-chain amino acid transamination — the metabolic step required for BCAAs to be utilized by muscle tissue. This ensures the 8.18 g of BCAAs per serving are efficiently processed rather than excreted. InstaClear™ Technology Aminocore uses ALLMAX's proprietary InstaClear™ BCAA processing technology to deliver a fully transparent, easy-mixing solution with no cloudy residue or sediment. Standard BCAA powders often clump or leave undissolved particles. InstaClear™ ensures complete solubility in cold water, making Aminocore suitable for sipping throughout training sessions.
