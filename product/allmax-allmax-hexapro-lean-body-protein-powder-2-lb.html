Hexapro's 6-protein matrix layers fast, medium, and slow-digesting proteins: Whey Protein Concentrate, Milk Protein Concentrate, Milk Protein Isolate, Whey Protein Isolate, Micellar Casein, and Whey Protein Hydrolysate. A study published in the American Journal of Physiology, Endocrinology, and Metabolism found that ingestion of a whey and casein blend led to a 74% longer retention of amino acids in the bloodstream during the later phase of digestion compared to whey alone—suggesting a blended approach may extend amino acid availability. ALLMAX Hexapro: High Protein Lean Meal for Muscle Growth and Recovery Protein timing matters, and the most neglected opportunity is the overnight period. During sleep, the body goes the longest period without protein intake. Research suggests consuming 20–40 g of sustained-release protein before bed may stimulate muscle protein synthesis and limit overnight muscle breakdown. A study published in Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise found that pre-sleep protein ingestion stimulated overnight muscle protein synthesis and improved whole-body protein balance. With its 8-hour release profile, Hexapro is formulated to address this gap, serving as a high-protein, lean meal at any time of day, including before sleep.
