ALLMAX Creatine Monohydrate: 99.9% Pure CreaSyn™, Micronized to 200 Mesh Allmax Creatine Monohydrate Powder provides 5 grams of pharmaceutical-grade creatine monohydrate per serving. Every batch is manufactured with CreaSyn™, a pharmaceutical-grade creatine, rigorously held to strict standards that meet or exceed 3rd-Party independent testing for WADA Banned Substances and cGMP guidelines. This micronized creatine powder dissolves rapidly and absorbs efficiently, with no fillers, sugar, or additives. Backed by over 500 peer-reviewed studies , creatine monohydrate has been shown to improve maximal power and strength by 5–15% and weightlifting endurance by up to 14% above placebo during short-term, high-intensity training. How Creatine Monohydrate Works Creatine monohydrate increases intramuscular stores of phosphocreatine, which the body uses to regenerate adenosine triphosphate (ATP) during high-intensity exercise. Higher ATP availability translates directly to greater power output, increased training volume, and faster recovery between sets. According to the International Society of Sports Nutrition (ISSN), creatine monohydrate is one of the most well-researched and effective ergogenic nutritional supplements available to athletes for increasing high-intensity exercise capacity and lean body mass.
