About this product

Engineered in partnership with TRX, the Aion x TRX Men’s Weighted Workout Vest is built to increase output from the movement you are already doing.

At the core is Dynamic Resistance™—Aion’s signature system combining compression, fractional weight, and neoprene insulation. Together, these elements elevate cardiovascular demand, increase muscular engagement, and enhance overall energy output—without requiring additional time, reps, or complexity.

The vest is tailored specifically for the male frame, offering a structured, body-contouring fit that stays secure through dynamic movement while preserving full range of motion. Weight is precisely calibrated by size, ranging from 7 to 12.52 lbs, allowing for progressive resistance without compromising balance or performance.

Constructed from premium neoprene with antimicrobial properties, the vest also delivers targeted compression and heat capture across the core—supporting posture, stamina, and active recovery.

Finished in a sleek black design with signature TRX yellow detailing and dual Aion + TRX branding, it represents the fusion of two performance-driven systems—built for those who expect more from every movement.

Product Specifications
  • Weight placement : Evenly balanced to move with your body during exercise
  • Fractional Load : 3-5% of body weight (weight distributed evenly across torso based on the size of the vest).
  • Durability : Tested by high-level athletes for materials/composition durability.
  • Cuts of Fabrics : Cut and sewn to fit the build and contours of female bodies without feeling bulky.
  • Compression : Neoprene conforms to your body, both stretching and retaining its shape.
  • Heat : Heat capture from neoprene insulation in strategic areas of the body.
  • Weights are encapsulated in foam to eliminate movement during exercise 
  • Weights are 1/2" Diameter 303 stainless steel discs
  • Weights have fabric around weight packs that are Biosmart 4.1 oz Antimicrobial Fabric
  • Stitching : Perma Core Sewing Thread
  • Sizing Guide
Vest Technology 

Crafted from durable Neoprene/Nylon Airprene and Antimicrobial materials, the Vest features strategic weight, compression, and heat capture in key areas of the body to promote active recovery, maintain stamina, and achieve maximum results.

For Best Use

For best use when training, running or hiking, we advise wearing a shirt under the vest to collect perspiration and provide the best comfort.
