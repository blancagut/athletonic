About this product

A tribute to Bruce Lee’s legendary philosophy, the Fairtex X Alma BJJ Gi captures the essence of water—adaptable, flowing, and powerful. Brazilian Jiu Jitsu, often called the “gentle art,” mirrors this mindset, embracing fluidity and intuition in every movement.  This co-branded Gi unites Thai and Japanese artistry in a conceptual design that reflects water’s elegance and strength. Crafted in Japan from premium Diamond Weave fabric, it features intricate embroidery symbolizing the constant, graceful motion of water. Perfect for practitioners who value both performance and philosophy. 

Features:

  • Concept-inspired design based on Bruce Lee’s “Be Like Water” philosophy
  • Premium Diamond Weave fabric for durability and comfort
  • Crafted in Japan with precision and care
  • Elegant embroidery depicting water’s flow and form
  • Fusion of Thai & Japanese art through a unique collaborative vision
