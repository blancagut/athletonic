Questions, answered.

What is the NOVOS Bar?

The NOVOS Bar is the first and only longevity bar, formulated to deliver complete, real-food nutrition in a convenient, science-backed format. Each bar contains up to 20 superfoods and five targeted longevity ingredients, including taurine, astaxanthin, and functional mushrooms. It’s designed to support steady energy, skin health, focus, and overall healthspan as part of your daily routine.

What makes the NOVOS Bar different from other bars?

Most bars focus on protein or calories. The NOVOS Bar is formulated as a daily longevity solution, providing:

  • Five targeted longevity compounds including taurine, astaxanthin, and adaptogenic mushroom extracts
  • Real-food micronutrients delivering up to 50% Daily Value of essential vitamins and minerals
  • A clean formula free from seed oils, soy, gluten, and artificial sweeteners
  • Expert development by leaders in longevity science, nutrition, and gastronomy

This combination makes it unlike standard protein bars, snack bars, or meal bars.

How does the NOVOS Bar support longevity?

The NOVOS Bar includes ingredients studied for their roles in supporting healthy cellular, mitochondrial, and metabolic pathways. These include:

  • Taurine – supports heart, brain, muscle, and mitochondrial health
  • Astaxanthin – supports skin hydration and elasticity and helps protect cellular structures
  • Lion’s Mane, Reishi & Cordyceps – support cognitive function, stress resilience, immunity, and endurance

All dosages are aligned with published research in humans and animals

Is the NOVOS Bar a snack or a meal?

It can be either:

  • 1 bar = a nutrient-dense, longevity-focused snack
  • 2 bars = a practical meal replacement or post-workout fuel

It’s designed to fit your lifestyle while providing meaningful nutrition and functional benefits.

When should I eat the NOVOS Bar?

Anytime you need complete, real-food nourishment, including:

  • Busy mornings or long workdays
  • Post-exercise recovery
  • Midday fuel
  • Travel or on-the-go situations
Can I take the NOVOS Bar with other NOVOS supplements?

Yes. The NOVOS Bar is designed to complement NOVOS Core, Vital, and Boost as part of a complete daily longevity stack.

Is the NOVOS Bar vegetarian, gluten-free, or allergen-friendly?

Yes. All flavors are vegetarian, gluten-free, soy-free, and free from seed oils.

  • Mixed Berry Crunch and Salted Chocolate Crunch are dairy-free and allergen-friendly.
  • The bars use sunflower and pumpkin seed butter, while only the Peanut Butter flavor contains peanuts.
  • All bars are made without artificial additives or tree nuts.
What ingredients are inside, and why?

Here are just a few highlights:

  • Taurine (1.5g) – Boosts energy, supports heart-brain-muscle systems, helps slow biological aging
  • Astaxanthin (3mg) – Improves skin health, protects mitochondria, combats oxidative stress
  • Lion’s Mane, Reishi, Cordyceps (200mg each) – Support cognitive function, immune resilience, and endurance
  • Pumpkin Seed (3g) and Sunflower Seed Butter (9g) – Help maintain heart, brain, hormonal, and metabolic health
  • Olive oil, honey, rosemary extract, fruits & veggies – Provide antioxidant protection, polyphenols, and long-term health benefits
Is it backed by research?

Absolutely. Every active ingredient and dose is selected based on over 450 scientific studies. For example:

  • Taurine extended lifespan by up to 12% in mice
  • Astaxanthin improved skin hydration and elasticity in humans, and extended lifespan in animals by ~10%
  • Mushroom β-glucans reduced cellular senescence markers by over 50% in preclinical models
  • All ingredients are Generally Recognized As Safe (GRAS) and independently tested.

You can explore the full body of research behind each ingredient on our ingredients page.

Does it taste good?

Absolutely. Developed in collaboration with a former Michelin-starred pastry chef and a leading nutrition expert, this bar strikes a balance between functional ingredients and satisfying taste and texture. It combines ingredients like cocoa, nut butters, and honey for natural sweetness, along with crisps for great texture. The result: smooth, subtly sweet, never chalky or artificial.

Is the NOVOS Bar safe for daily use?

Yes. The NOVOS Bar is designed for daily consumption, typically 1–2 bars per day. All ingredients are well-tolerated, food-based, and used at levels consistent with published research.

Is the NOVOS Bar safe during pregnancy or breastfeeding?

The NOVOS Bar is made from food-based, GRAS ingredients. However, because pregnancy and breastfeeding involve unique nutritional needs, we recommend consulting your healthcare provider before adding any new supplement or functional food to your routine.

Is the NOVOS Bar low in sugar or low glycemic?

The NOVOS Bar is sweetened naturally with honey and real-food ingredients rather than refined sugars or artificial sweeteners. It’s designed to provide steady, sustained energy without the crash associated with high-sugar snacks. Exact sugar content is listed in the Nutrition Facts panel for full transparency.

What are the benefits of the NOVOS Bar?

The NOVOS Bar delivers real-food nutrition with functional longevity ingredients designed to support daily energy, focus, skin health, and overall wellness. It offers a convenient way to fuel your day with superfoods, essential nutrients, and targeted longevity compounds—whether as a snack or a meal replacement.

Does the NOVOS Bar support weight management?

The NOVOS Bar can be used as part of a balanced routine because it replaces less nutritious snacks with real-food ingredients and functional longevity nutrients. It isn’t designed specifically for weight loss, but one bar can serve as a satisfying snack and two bars as a convenient meal replacement. Your overall eating pattern will determine whether it supports your personal weight goals.

Do you ship the NOVOS Bar internationally?

Yes. NOVOS ships the NOVOS Bar to many countries worldwide. Shipping times vary by location, customs processing and time of year, but most international orders arrive within 7–10 business days. Read more about our shipping policy.

What is your return policy for the NOVOS Bar?

If you purchased the NOVOS Bar (or any NOVOS product) and wish to return it, U.S. returns must be made within 30 days of purchase and the product must be unopened. Standard return fees apply including a return-shipping deduction and a restocking fee. For international orders, all sales are final. Read more about our return policy.
