Odor-controlled softgels sourced from wild-caught anchovies and sardines 180 Softgels Per Bottle . 60-90 day supply at the recommended 2–3 softgel daily dose Why Omega-3 Supplementation Matters The standard North American diet tends to contain far more Omega-6 fatty acids relative to Omega-3 fatty acids, a cellular imbalance associated with reduced inflammatory balance. Since the body cannot produce EPA or DHA on its own, these essential fatty acids must be obtained through diet or supplementation. For athletes and active individuals who don't regularly consume cold-water fish, a high-quality omega-3 fish oil supplement is a practical, daily way to restore this balance and support overall wellness. Molecular Distillation & Purity Process ALLMAX uses molecular-level distillation in a cGMP-registered facility to strip environmental contaminants from the fish oil concentrate. Each batch is tested for heavy metal contamination: mercury, arsenic, cadmium, and lead, so you get all the benefits of omega-3 fatty acids without the toxin concerns that can come with whole fish consumption. The formula is enclosed in an opaque container to protect against light-source degradation, preserving potency and freshness. EPA & DHA: The Key Omega-3 Fatty Acids EPA (eicosapentaenoic acid) and DHA (docosahexaenoic acid) are the long-chain omega-3 fatty acids the body can use directly, with no conversion required. EPA plays a primary role in supporting heart health and maintaining the body's healthy inflammatory response, while DHA is a major structural component of brain tissue essential for nerve conduction, memory, and cognitive function. Together, they form the foundation of any comprehensive omega-3 supplementation strategy. Discover how omega-3 fatty acids support joint health and mobility . IFOS 5-Star Certification ALLMAX Omega-3 is manufactured with an IFOS (International Fish Oil Standards) 5-Star Rating, a rigorous third-party certification that verifies the fish oil meets strict standards for purity, potency, freshness, and heavy-metal testing. For athletes and individuals who prioritize transparency and verified quality, the IFOS 5-Star Rating provides an additional layer of confidence beyond cGMP manufacturing.
