Each 2-capsule serving delivers seven active ingredients: TamaFlex, OptiMSM, 3-LASTIN Boswellia extract, Curcumin, White Willow Bark, and antioxidants Vitamins C & E. Key Features at a Glance: TamaFlex™ (225 mg) : a patented plant-based blend of Tamarind ( Tamarindus indica ) seed extract standardized to 90% proanthocyanidins and Turmeric ( Curcuma longa ) rhizome extract standardized to 15% curcuminoids OptiMSM® (450 mg) : premium methylsulfonylmethane for joint mobility and connective tissue support 3-LASTIN™ Boswellia extract (200 mg) : standardized to 70% boswellic acids Curcumin, Curcuma longa powder (100 mg) : antioxidant support for joint tissue White Willow Bark, Salix alba powder (100 mg) : supports daily joint comfort Vitamin C (60 mg, 67% DV) and Vitamin E (30 mg, 200% DV) : antioxidant protection for connective tissue Vegan capsules (Hypromellose) Rapid-release + 12-hour sustained-release delivery system Just 2 capsules once daily cGMP-registered facility, lab-tested every lot TamaFlex™: The Botanical Cornerstone of AllFlex The cornerstone of AllFlex is TamaFlex™, a patented, plant-based blend of Tamarind ( Tamarindus indica ) seed extract and Turmeric ( Curcuma longa ) rhizome extract. This standardized botanical complex is included to support joint comfort, mobility, and a healthy inflammatory response. Each serving delivers 225 mg of TamaFlex™. What Makes AllFlex One of the Best Supplements for Joint Health? Single-ingredient joint supplements address only one mechanism. AllFlex takes a multi-pathway approach combining distinct ingredient categories that work together to support joint health from multiple angles : TamaFlex™ (225 mg): The patented Tamarind and Turmeric botanical blend at the heart of AllFlex, standardized to 90% proanthocyanidins and 15% curcuminoids to support joint comfort, mobility, and a healthy inflammatory response. OptiMSM® (450 mg): A premium, distilled form of methylsulfonylmethane (MSM) that supports joint mobility, connective tissue health, and post-exercise recovery. 3-LASTIN™ Boswellia extract (200 mg): Standardized to 70% boswellic acids to help maintain a healthy inflammatory response associated with exercise and support comfortable joint movement. Curcumin, Curcuma longa powder (100 mg): Provides antioxidant protection and additional joint comfort support. White Willow Bark, Salix alba powder (100 mg): A botanical traditionally used to support joint comfort during physical activity. Vitamins C & E: Vitamin C (60 mg, 67% DV) and Vitamin E (30 mg, 200% DV) help protect cells from oxidative stress, supporting healthy connective tissues and long-term joint wellness. Antioxidant Support for Long-Term Joint Health Vitamins C and E, along with the antioxidant compounds in Curcumin, help protect joint tissue and connective structures from oxidative stress, a key factor in maintaining long-term joint comfort and mobility for athletes who train consistently. Dual-Action Delivery System: Rapid-Release + 12-Hour Sustained Support AllFlex uses an innovative delivery system: a rapid-release capsule for immediate absorption, followed by a sustained-release formulation that continues to work for up to 12 hours. This means joint support throughout your training day and into recovery, especially important when you understand how muscle damage impacts bodybuilding progress .
