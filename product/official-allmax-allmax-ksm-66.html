Scientifically Studied Stress & Cortisol Support Research on KSM-66 suggests it may support the body's stress response at a hormonal level. In one double-blind, placebo-controlled study involving 64 healthy adults, participants taking 300 mg of KSM-66 twice daily experienced a reduction in serum cortisol levels after 8 weeks, along with improvements in self-reported measures of stress, anxiety, and mood balance. As always, results may vary, and consistent daily use over 6–8 weeks is recommended to assess full adaptogenic benefits.
