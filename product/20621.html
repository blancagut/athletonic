About this product

Baseline 2.0 BJJ Gi


Elevate your game. Engineered for serious athletes who demand both performance and style, the Baseline 2.0 delivers high-end features with a clean, modern look.
Key Features:
  • Premium 350 gsm jacket—durable yet lightweight for movement and speed.
  • Athletic, tapered cut—designed to follow the body silhouette without restricting motion.
  • Soft, comfortable cotton twill pants—built for all-day wear, training after training.
  • Clean aesthetic with refined detailing—fits into both competition and lifestyle settings seamlessly.
Why It Matters:
Whether you’re drilling, rolling, or stepping onto the competition mat, this gi gives you the build to perform and the cut to look sharp. The 350 gsm jacket provides sturdiness for grips and durability, while the athletic taper means less bulk and better fit across the shoulders and chest. Meanwhile, the cotton twill pants bring comfort and flexibility—no compromise. The updated look keeps it modern and crisp, so you’re ready to show up in style.

Who It’s For:
  • Training partners who want a reliable build that won’t wear out after a few months.
  • Jiujiteiros who appreciate a minimalist aesthetic but still demand quality.
Fit & Care:
  • Available in full sizes (A0 – A5, etc.).
  • Athletic cut means slightly closer fit; if you prefer more room in the chest or midsection, consider sizing up.
  • Pre-wash cold and hang dry to maintain fabric integrity and finish. Avoid heavy tumble drying.
  • For best results: wash inside-out, mild detergent, no fabric softener.
Why Baseline 2.0?
You don’t need flashy graphics to perform. What you need is strength in every seam, freedom in every movement, and style in every roll. The Baseline line is built on that principle—simple but serious. And now, with the 2.0 update, you get an elevated look without sacrificing the trusted features. Train hard. Compete harder.
