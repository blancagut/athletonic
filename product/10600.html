Schiek Model 3004 Power Lifting Belt 💪

The Schiek Model 3004 Power Lifting Belt delivers unparalleled support, durability, and style for serious lifters. Combining exclusive Schiek features with an extra layer of neoprene-coated nylon reinforcement, this belt provides the stability and protection needed for heavy lifting. Proudly made in the USA, it’s built to last through the toughest training sessions.

Key Features with Exclusive Schiek Benefits:

  1. The Schiek Downward Angle Design 🔽 Schiek’s unique downward angle design naturally follows the contour of your back for optimal support and comfort. This shape ensures the belt stays in place during lifts without riding up.

  2. Unique Schiek Tapered Hip & Rib Contour 🛡️ Features Schiek’s signature hip and rib contour for a custom fit, eliminating pinching or discomfort while allowing for greater mobility during squats, deadlifts, and presses.

  3. Reinforced Neoprene-Coated Nylon 🧱 Added layer of neoprene-coated nylon makes this belt stiffer than the Model 2004, providing superior rigidity and durability for maximum support.

  4. Signature Schiek One-Way Velcro Closure 🎯 Schiek’s original one-way Velcro system ensures a secure and exact fit every time, with no in-between sizing.

  5. Dual Support Panels ✨ Designed with dual-reinforced support panels to enhance back stability and reduce stress on the spine.

  6. Stainless Steel Slide Bar Buckle 🔒 Durable stainless steel slide bar buckle locks the belt securely in place, ensuring it won’t pop open during heavy lifts.

  7. Wide Front for Abdominal Support 📏 Features an extra-wide front panel to provide maximum intra-abdominal pressure, stabilizing your core and reducing the risk of injury.

  8. Washable and Odor-Resistant 🧼 Made with easy-to-clean materials that resist odor buildup, keeping your belt fresh and ready for every workout.

  9. Customizable Strap Colors 🎨 Available with strap color options in Black, Blue, Pink, or Red, letting you personalize your gear to match your style.

  10. 2-Year Warranty 🛠️ Backed by a 2-year warranty, reflecting Schiek’s commitment to quality and durability.

Unique Schiek Advantages:

  • USA-Made Quality: Meticulously crafted in the USA, delivering superior craftsmanship and long-lasting performance.
  • Trusted by Athletes Worldwide: Schiek has been a go-to brand for over 30 years, trusted by powerlifters, bodybuilders, and strength athletes.
  • Precision Engineering: Every design element, from the Velcro closure to the hip contour, is tailored to provide unparalleled support and comfort for lifters.
