Product overview

Performance starts with the right formula.

Why Choose Allmax Carbion+ for Training Fuel and Hydration? Allmax Carbion+ is a zero-sugar carbohydrate and electrolyte formula that delivers 25 g of phased-release carbs per serving with a full electrolyte hydration blend of sodium (190 mg), potassium (100 mg), magnesium (60 mg), and calcium (30 mg). With Zero Sugar and zero bloating, Allmax Carbion+ is designed for pre-, intra-, and post-workout use — supporting steady energy, hydration balance, and training endurance without the sugar spikes or crashes of conventional sports drinks. Allmax Carbion+ features 3 proprietary blends: the Phased Delivery Energy Blend (25 g of Cyclo-D Highly Branched Cyclic Dextrin + PER4ORM maltodextrin + AWM2300 waxy maize + CocoH2 coconut water), the Absorption Blend (Prickly Pear, Bitter Melon, Cinnulin PF), and the Electrolyte Hydration Blend. This multi-stage approach supports gradual carbohydrate uptake, enhanced nutrient absorption, and sustained electrolyte replenishment. What Allmax Carbion+ Does: Energy, Hydration & Recovery Refresh: Allmax Carbion+ mixes easily into a clear, refreshing drink with zero bloating. Hydrate: Provides key electrolytes to support hydration and help maintain performance during training. Perform: The phased-delivery carbohydrate blend is designed to provide fuel throughout your workout without the crash associated with sugar-based sports drinks. Recover: Carbohydrates play an important role in recovery and are essential for hard-working athletes. Phased-Delivery Carbohydrate Blend for Steady Energy Allmax Carbion+ delivers 25 g of carbohydrates per serving through a phased-delivery system: 12 g of Cyclo-D Highly Branched Cyclic Dextrin (HBCD) for fast gastric emptying and rapid glucose delivery, 12 g of PER4ORM 4-Form Phased Release Maltodextrin for sustained energy, 1,000 mg of AWM2300 Ultra-High Molecular Weight Waxy Maize for extended glycogen support, and 400 mg of CocoH2 Concentrated Coconut Water for natural electrolytes. This phased approach supports gradual carbohydrate uptake without the sugar spikes or energy crashes of single-source carb drinks. Ultra-Refreshing, Zero-Sugar Hydration Formula Allmax Carbion+ mixes instantly into a light electrolyte drink with zero sugar. The Cyclo-D HBCD and PER4ORM maltodextrin blend is highly soluble, producing a clear, non-bloating drink even at full serving strength. This makes Allmax Carbion+ effective during sessions lasting 45+ minutes when athletes need sustained hydration without digestive discomfort. Complete Electrolyte Support for Active Performance Allmax Carbion+ includes the Electrolyte Hydration Blend with 190 mg sodium (as sodium bicarbonate, sodium citrate, sodium chloride), 100 mg potassium (as potassium bicarbonate, potassium citrate), 60 mg magnesium (as magnesium citrate, magnesium gluconate), and 30 mg calcium (as calcium citrate, calcium 5-hydrate). These bicarbonate and citrate forms support absorption and fluid balance during intense exercise. Absorption Blend for Enhanced Nutrient Uptake Allmax Carbion+ includes a proprietary Absorption Blend with 108 mg Prickly Pear Cactus Extract, 56 mg Bitter Melon Extract, and 15 mg Cinnulin PF Cinnamon Bark Extract. These ingredients support glucose uptake into muscle cells, helping to shuttle carbohydrates where they are needed most during training. Training Support for Strength, Endurance, and Recovery Allmax Carbion+ supports glycogen replenishment and hydration before, during, or after exercise. The 25 g phased-delivery carbohydrate system is suitable for strength training, endurance sessions, HIIT, and competition-day carb loading — delivering measurable carbohydrate intake without sugar or GI heaviness. Convenient and Stack-Friendly Allmax Carbion+ is available in Blue Ice, Lemon Lime, Pineapple Mango, and Unflavored — making it stackable with Allmax Aminocore BCAAs , ALLMAX Impact Igniter 2.0 , or EAAs. The Unflavored version blends seamlessly into any flavored supplement without altering taste. Scoop-Lock technology keeps the scoop at the top of the container for easy access.
