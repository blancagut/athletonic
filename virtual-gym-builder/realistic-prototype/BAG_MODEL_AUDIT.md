## Geometry and acceptance notes

- No heavy bag uses `capsuleGeometry`; revolution bodies use metric `LatheGeometry` points.
- No bag uses non-uniform object scale to correct dimensions.
- All bag shells, UC1 pads, and Maddox padded surfaces use the same deterministic unbranded leather bump texture with restrained clearcoat and non-metallic response.
- Cylindrical bags have nearly flat lower caps, a small perimeter radius, straight walls, upper shoulders, profile-conforming cap and panel seams, discrete flat webbing, D-rings, interlocking chain, hook, and beam.
- Bowling, uppercut, angle, teardrop, and water bags use multi-point lower-cap curves that compress gradually toward the center. HB11 retains a small nearly flat central contact area from its reference; the other shaped bags finish in visibly rounded filled-leather bases rather than planar discs.
- HB12 and HB13 have the maximum-diameter shoulder above the narrow trunk. HB12 retains its lower tether; the photo-verified HB13 hangs freely.
- Double-end bags connect directly to floor and ceiling anchors without a rack or freestanding beam. Speed bags connect to a two-part swivel under a circular drum.
- The selected-object path displays the exact 1.80 m neutral human and metric ruler for every model. The reference is offset in `+X/-Z` so it remains beside, rather than behind, the object in perspective and lateral views.
